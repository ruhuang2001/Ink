## Design Context

### Users
Ink is a self-hosted personal workspace for people who collect, refine, and print small pieces of content on Memobird thermal printers. They use it on desktop and mobile to move quickly between conversation, print review, device setup, and occasional administration.

### Brand Personality
Calm, tactile, and quietly modern. The product should feel like a thoughtful writing desk connected to a small paper printer: focused and personable, never corporate, flashy, or overly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a refined editorial “digital paper” direction with warm neutrals, strong typography, generous whitespace, fine dividers, and restrained amber accents. Prefer open layouts and list-like structures over repeated rounded cards. Support both light and dark themes. Avoid generic dashboard grids, excessive pills, glassmorphism, gradients used for decoration, and card-heavy AI-generated aesthetics.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the primary journey obvious: conversation, printing, and settings only.
2. Use hierarchy, spacing, and dividers before adding containers or decoration.
3. Treat settings as a calm secondary workspace with clear local navigation.
4. Preserve tactile warmth while keeping controls modern, compact, and accessible.
5. Adapt the composition for mobile instead of merely shrinking the desktop layout.
